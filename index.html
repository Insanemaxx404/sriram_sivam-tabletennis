About the Player

Player Profile

Sriram Sivam is a dynamic left-handed offensive shakehand player from Tamil Nadu and one of India's most exciting young table tennis talents on the rise. Built on a "first-attack" mentality, his game is defined by explosive speed, technical precision and relentless initiative.

His standout weapon is a devastating backhand banana flick that instantly neutralises short serves and puts opponents on the back foot — before he closes points with high-rotation forehand topspin loops using sharp pivot footwork.

Tactically astute with a varied serve repertoire, he dictates rally tempo from close to the table and excels in high-speed counter-topspin exchanges. A high-energy competitor with an infectious winning mentality, Sriram is a proven performer at both national and international levels.

⚡ Banana Flick
Neutralises short serves with an explosive over-the-table backhand flick, immediately seizing the initiative.
🔄 Pivot & Loop
Uses step-around footwork to cover the backhand corner with his forehand, executing high-rotation topspin kills.
🎯 Third-Ball Attack
Serves short to force weak returns, immediately following up with a powerful forehand attack.
🧠 Tactical Serves
Employs high-toss and short pendulum serves designed to produce specific returns he can punish offensively.
